# SkyWings Airlines — Passenger Rules & Regulations

Document ID: SWA-POL-2026-01 | Effective: 01 January 2026 | Version 3.2

## 1. Baggage Allowance

Economy class passengers may carry 7 kg cabin baggage (55×35×25 cm) and 15 kg checked baggage. Premium class allows 10 kg cabin and 25 kg checked. Business class allows 12 kg cabin baggage (2 pieces) and 35 kg checked baggage.

Excess baggage is charged at ₹550 / $7 per kg at the airport, or ₹450 per kg if pre-purchased online at least 6 hours before departure. One personal item (laptop bag or handbag, up to 3 kg) is allowed free in addition to cabin baggage.

## 2. Check-in & Boarding

Web check-in opens 48 hours and closes 60 minutes before departure. Airport check-in counters close 45 minutes before domestic and 75 minutes before international departures. Boarding gates close 20 minutes before departure; passengers arriving later are treated as no-shows. A valid government-issued photo ID (passport for international travel) is mandatory for all passengers above 12 years.

## 3. Cancellations & Refunds

Cancellation fees depend on time before departure: more than 72 hours — ₹1,500 / $18 per sector; 72 to 24 hours — ₹2,500 / $30 per sector; 24 to 4 hours — ₹3,500 / $42 per sector; under 4 hours or no-show — only taxes are refunded.

Refunds are processed to the original payment method within 7 business days. Fully refundable "Flexi" fares are exempt from cancellation fees up to 2 hours before departure.

## 4. Flight Changes & No-show

Date or time changes are permitted up to 4 hours before departure for a fee of ₹2,000 / $24 per sector plus fare difference. Same-day rebooking after a missed flight is available within 6 hours at 50% of the applicable fare, subject to seat availability. Name changes are not permitted; name corrections (up to 3 characters) are free once.

## 5. Special Assistance

Wheelchair support is free; request at booking or at least 48 hours before departure. Unaccompanied minors (5–12 years) may travel with the UM service at ₹3,000 per sector; a guardian declaration form is mandatory. Pets (dogs or cats up to 5 kg including cage) may travel in-cabin on domestic flights at ₹4,000 per sector; advance approval is required. Expectant mothers may fly up to the 32nd week of pregnancy with a fit-to-fly certificate issued within 7 days of travel.

## 6. Prohibited Items

Not permitted in cabin or checked baggage: explosives, fireworks, compressed gases, flammable liquids, and corrosives. Power banks are not allowed in checked baggage. Power banks (up to 20,000 mAh) and e-cigarettes may be carried in cabin baggage only. Sharp objects and tools over 6 cm must be checked in.

## 7. Delays & Compensation

For delays of 2 to 4 hours, passengers receive meals and refreshments. For delays of 4 to 6 hours, passengers receive a meal voucher plus ₹2,000 travel credit. For delays over 6 hours, passengers are entitled to a full refund or free rebooking, plus hotel accommodation if the delay is overnight. For cancellations notified less than 24 hours before departure, passengers are entitled to a full refund or a free alternative flight.

## 8. Contact & Escalation Departments

Baggage Services handles lost, delayed, or damaged baggage — baggage@skywings.example. Refunds Desk handles refund status and cancellation disputes — refunds@skywings.example. Special Assistance handles wheelchair, unaccompanied minor, pet, and medical queries — care@skywings.example. General Support handles all other queries — support@skywings.example. Unresolved queries raised via the AI assistant are automatically escalated by email to the relevant department with the full conversation context.
